Our mission

Make hands-on STEM learning more accessible.

FutureForge Initiative is focused on charitable and educational STEM opportunities that help people explore engineering, build practical skills, and contribute to their communities.

Our interests include engineering, computer-aided design (CAD), robotics, 3D printing, digital fabrication, hands-on learning, mentoring, and community service. We aim to connect these fields to projects that are useful, educational, and community-minded.
