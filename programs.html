Programs & focus areas

Learning that leads somewhere.

We connect creative technical learning with projects that can make a difference in everyday life.

✳⚙
01

Design for people

Accessible Making

Digital fabrication can turn small ideas into useful objects. This focus area explores 3D-printed fidgets, sensory tools, and other thoughtfully designed items that may support engagement and hands-on exploration.

We aim to listen to the people and organizations we serve, prioritize usability, and improve designs through feedback.

3D printing · Design
02

Build confidence

Young Engineers

Engineering is best understood by trying it. We support opportunities for young people to explore CAD, prototyping, robotics, and design thinking through guided, hands-on activities.

Learning can include sketching a solution, building a model, testing what works, and iterating based on results.

CAD · STEM education
03

Learn by building

Robotics & Makers

Robotics brings mechanics, electronics, design, and teamwork together. This focus area aims to strengthen maker learning by sharing knowledge, encouraging collaboration, and supporting access to practical resources.

We welcome conversations with schools, teams, educators, and community groups about needs and potential projects.

Robotics · Mentoring
04

Put skills to work

Community Giving

Community service can connect learning with local needs. Potential activities include educational-resource donations, book drives, STEM demonstrations, maker projects, and volunteer support for schools, libraries, and community organizations.

We seek collaborations that are practical, respectful, and aligned with our educational and charitable mission.

Service · Partnerships
